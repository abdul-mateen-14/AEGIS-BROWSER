Aegis Shield

Ad blocking lives in the network service,
not in an extension.

A small C++ engine inside Chromium's network layer inspects every subresource request and drops third-party connections to known ad and tracker hosts with ERR_BLOCKED_BY_CLIENT — the same signal adblock extensions produce, without installing anything.

The blocklist is generated from EasyList and EasyPrivacy and compiled into the build. Browser-initiated and same-party requests always pass, and the Aegis sync and ecosystem domains are hard-allowlisted, so the browser can never break itself.

Design, scope and policy: docs/SHIELD.md · semantics-tested blocklist, generated by scripts/aegis-shield/gen_blocklist.py
