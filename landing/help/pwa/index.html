Web apps & device connections

Aegis treats web apps and connected devices exactly like Chromium does: nothing starts, installs or connects until you explicitly allow it.

Installing web apps

Any site built as a Progressive Web App can be installed. On Android use Menu → Add to Home screen; on desktop click the install icon in the omnibox. Installed apps get their own window and launcher entry and behave like native apps — there is no app store, no review step and no background service you did not approve.

Cast, Bluetooth, USB, serial and HID

When a site wants to reach hardware — casting a tab, pairing a Bluetooth device, talking to a USB, serial or HID gadget — Aegis shows a picker dialog and nothing connects until you choose a device. These permissions are per-site and can be revoked anytime under Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings.

None of this is coupled to a Google account: casting works over your local network and device pairing happens entirely between your machine and the device.
