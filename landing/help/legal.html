Section 2

Privacy Policy

Plain-language privacy. Last updated: November 2026.

  • What the Aegis project collects: nothing. No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reports to us, no accounts tied to tracking, no advertising identifiers.
  • What the browser sends by default: the minimum needed for a working modern browser — DNS/DHCP for networking, certificate revocation checks, and Safe Browsing protection at the standard Chromium level (adjustable in chrome://settings/security).
  • Search: Google Search is the default search engine. When you search, your query goes to Google — exactly as it would if you typed it on google.com. This is a search-engine choice you can change at any time; it is not a data-sharing arrangement with the Aegis project.
  • Google services: no Google service is switched on by default: no Google-account sign-in integration, no Gemini/AI features, no Google-synced password storage, no usage metrics sent to Google. Sites you deliberately visit (including gemini.google.com or accounts.google.com) behave like any other website.
  • Sync: if you enable Aegis Sync, your data is encrypted on your device before it leaves; the sync server (an inspectable, open-source Cloudflare Worker) stores opaque ciphertext and your email address only. See the Help: Aegis ID & Sync section for the exact threat model.
  • Passwords: Aegis Passwords are stored locally, encrypted. They are never uploaded unless you switch on Aegis Sync yourself.
  • Questions: open an issue at github.com/abdul-mateen-14/AEGIS-BROWSER/issues ↗.
