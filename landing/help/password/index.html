Passwords in Aegis

Aegis includes Chromium’s password manager, rebranded and kept local: passwords are stored encrypted on your device. There is no Google account involved.

  • Save and autofill passwords as usual.
  • Turn on Aegis Sync with your Aegis ID to sync them across devices — end-to-end encrypted, so the server only ever stores ciphertext.
  • Check Settings → Passwords to review saved items.
